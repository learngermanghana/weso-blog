Our reunion with Juliet was emotional—happy to see her, but heartbroken that she has to go through treatment again.

## Juliet's Story: Then and Now

📷 *Slide to see our first meeting in 2019 and now.*

After a courageous battle involving six rounds of chemotherapy, extensive surgery, and additional chemotherapy, Juliet reached a place of hope.

She completed treatment, received a prosthetic eye, and enjoyed three years of remission. She returned to school with a fresh start.

Just days into the new school term this year, a rapid-growing swelling appeared. Even though she never missed her quarterly check-ups, we received devastating news: **the cancer has returned—larger and more aggressive than before.**

## The Current Situation

Juliet is back at **Korle-Bu Teaching Hospital**, fighting once again.

- **Current status:** Ongoing chemotherapy to shrink the tumor.
- **Next steps:** Additional chemotherapy cycles, followed by major surgery.
- **Financial need:** **Over GH₵10,000** for specialized medications, advanced scans, and surgery.
